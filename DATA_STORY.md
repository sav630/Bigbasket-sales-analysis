
BigBasket Data Story
Overview

The dashboard shows BigBasket's delivered revenue performance from January to June 2026. The total revenue across all categories is INR 88,282 from 434 delivered orders. The dashboard compares each category's revenue with its target and shows which categories are above or below target.

Category Performance
Above Target

Household Essentials generated INR 21,715 against a target of INR 17,000. It is INR 4,715 above target.

Personal Care generated INR 16,382 against a target of INR 15,500. It is INR 882 above target.

Bakery generated INR 15,410 against a target of INR 12,000. It is INR 3,410 above target.

Three out of the six categories are meeting or exceeding their targets.

Below Target - Watch

Dairy & Eggs generated INR 14,090 against a target of INR 16,500. It is INR 2,410 below target, which puts it in the Below Target - Watch group.

Below Target - Critical

Snacks & Beverages generated INR 10,895 against a target of INR 13,000. It is INR 2,105 below target.

Fruits & Vegetables generated INR 9,790 against a target of INR 12,000. It is INR 2,210 below target.

These two categories are more than 15% below their targets, so they are classified as Below Target - Critical.

Recommendations

Invest more catalog and marketing effort in Household Essentials. This category has the highest revenue at INR 21,715 and is INR 4,715 above its INR 17,000 target. It has the strongest revenue performance in the dashboard, so it is a category where additional effort can build on an already strong result.

Review Snacks & Beverages and Fruits & Vegetables with the category and supplier teams. Snacks & Beverages is INR 2,105 below its INR 13,000 target, while Fruits & Vegetables is INR 2,210 below its INR 12,000 target. Both are classified as Below Target - Critical, so these categories should be reviewed before additional investment is made.

Summary

Overall, three of the six categories are above target, while three categories are below target. Household Essentials has the highest revenue and the largest positive variance. Fruits & Vegetables and Snacks & Beverages have the largest performance gaps among the categories classified as critical. The dashboard provides a simple view of revenue, delivered orders, average order value, and category target performance for January through June 2026.
